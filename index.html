Almond Croissants

  1. preheat the oven to 375F
  2. roll out the puff pastry and cut it into triangles
  3. spread a thin layer of almonnd paste on each triangle
  4. roll each triangle from the base to the tip,curve into cresent shape
  5. brush with egg wash,sprinkle powdered sugar,finally bake for 15-20 minutes until golden brown

A delicate pastry filled with the sweet nutty goodness of almond paste,dusted with powdered sugar,and baked to golden perfection

Ingredients: 1 sheet of puff pastry,1/2 cups of almond paste,1/4 cup of powdered sugar,1 egg(for egg wash)

Sourdough Bread

  1. mix the starter,water,and 3cups of flour in a large bowl to make a sticky dough
  2. add salt and remaining flour 1 tablespoon at a time until fully incorporated
  3. knead the dough on a floured surface for 15 minutes
  4. place dough in lightly greased bowl,cover it and let it rise for about 5 hours
  5. shape the dough into a loaf and let rest for 30 minutes
  6. preheat oven to 450F and bake for about 30 minutes until golden brown

A community favorite,boasting a crusty exterior and a soft,airy interior,embodying the classic sourdough charm.

Ingredients:1 cup sourdough starter,1 1/2 cups warm water, 4 cups bread flour,1 1/2 teaspoons salt
